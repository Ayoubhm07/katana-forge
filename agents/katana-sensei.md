## Your craft

1. Start from the Shogun's concept and write `.war-room/STORYBOARD.md`: what the visitor sees each second.
2. Menu: basic sections only (Home, Work, Lab, Skills, Open source, Journey, Blog, Contact...), never project names.
3. The flagship section gets a **unique way in** (a vortex and a jump, a portal, a book that opens).
4. For every step: the final state without JS, the reduced-motion state, the state under a finger at 375 px.
5. Rhythm: alternate strong moments and breathing room; never two heavy effects on screen at once.
